# Style references

Reels Olga sent as references, watched with the vidIQ connector (Nov 2026). Notes are what the
analysis reported; font names are guesses from the look, not confirmed.

## Editing styles

**DfDEBsuPtrH / DgFa5Z2NJgC — "editorial gold" talking head (same creator)**
- Captions: lowercase bold geometric sans, white, key words in soft gold (~#E5C158); 1–3 words at a time,
  chest height, small spring pop. Accent words switch to an italic serif.
- Hook at top: small white sans lines around one big gold italic-serif phrase ("6 posts").
- Graphics: white rounded cards with soft shadows; numbered 6-card grid with line icons, one card
  highlighted yellow; fill-in-the-blank prompt cards; row of press logos; profile badge with a cursor
  clicking Follow; lead-magnet page mockup on the CTA.
- Notebook mode (DgFa): speaker cut out and pinned bottom-centre over a full-screen lined-paper page; an
  animated pencil writes headings, ticks boxes, crosses out, circles; yellow highlighter wipes; page scrolls
  up per point; photo stickers with labels ("3 kids"); zoom out to the full page + bookmark icon.
- Sound: quiet lo-fi bed; pencil scratch, page whoosh, soft clicks/pops on each graphic.
- Pacing: something changes every ~0.5–1.2 s; punch-in 1.0→1.15 between points.

**DeSbNv9BeTi — "crisp caps"**
- Captions: bold ALL-CAPS sans, white with gold active word, 2–4 words.
- Title at top: cream bold condensed caps ("MOST ASKED QUESTION"); numbered headings in a script font
  ("1. Back Camera"); bullet facts as small caps at top.
- One giant word at a time over the face for the punchline ("CLEAN / YOUR / CAMERA / LENS").
- Collage of comment screenshots popping in; phone screen recording as a rounded card with big beige caps
  text behind it.

**DgF9j3qu9bL — b-roll voiceover reel**
- Voiceover over lifestyle clips, hard cuts every 1.5–3 s, alternating aspirational shots with proof
  (screen recordings, dashboards, floating stat cards).
- Captions centred and large, stacked lines of mixed sizes, lowercase sans, white with pale gold (~#FFE484)
  on numbers and key words. Counter pill top-right ("1/4").
- Her workflow as shown: Claude writes hook options + a ~45 s voiceover, picks shots from the camera
  roll, and a storyboard page lists script, shots and an "Approve reel" button.

## Dashboards (both are Claude artifacts)
**Df40xLNJDJx — monthly social plan.** Month title + counts (posts / approved / to review); calendar with
post thumbnails; Instagram grid preview; left list of posts by week; centre post view with preview,
caption editor, date/time, "Approve as is" / "Request changes"; right "to do this month" checklist.
Cream background, plum accent, serif headings + sans body + monospace labels.

**DgFdl-3Cv4u — weekly board.** Seven day columns; cards for Reel / Story / Email / Carousel / Trial reel
with thumbnail and time; "N of 28 placed" counter. Warm neutrals, clean sans.

## Olga's own fonts
Mental Offload uses Fraunces (serif, with italics) + Inter. That pair matches the "editorial gold" look.

**DfA4WSbBMVM — "Weekly Content Studio" (Claude artifact).** One working page per week.
- Header: week switcher (← Oct 26 – Nov 1 →, "This week"), "Launch week" toggle, saved indicator.
- Tabs: This week · Film · Series · Format library · What's working.
- This week: pipeline counters (Idea 7/7 · Scripted 1/7 · Filmed 0/7 · Scheduled 0/7 · Posted 0/7), content
  day picker, the offer keywords point to, this week's focus.
- Series: one column per recurring series (format, length, job), "next up" episode, an idea bank per series
  with tick boxes and "send to this week".
- Format library: ~22 reusable post formats as cards, filter by job (Attract / Deepen trust / Start
  conversations / Sell) and type (Reel / Carousel / Quote); each has a scripting note, hook examples,
  "Open scripting view", "Copy hook".
- Look: cream background, white cards with thin borders, serif headings + sans UI, black pills, mustard tags.
- Reel's own captions: serif, pastel gold with white, centred, word by word.
