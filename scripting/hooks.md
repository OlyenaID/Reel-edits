# Hook patterns (fallback)

Only used when Olga's `reel-script` skill isn't available. Her skill's hooks come first.

A hook does three jobs in under two seconds: says who it's for, promises something, and opens a loop the
viewer needs closed. Fill the [brackets]. Mix patterns across a week so the feed doesn't feel samey.

## Call out the viewer
1. If you're a [who] who [situation], this is for you.
2. [Who], stop scrolling, you need to hear this.
3. This is for the [who] who [secret struggle].
4. POV: you're a [who] and [relatable moment].
5. Every [who] I know does this, and it's costing them [thing].

## Mistakes and warnings
6. Stop [common action] if you want [result].
7. The [number] mistakes keeping you from [result].
8. You're [doing X] wrong, and it's not your fault.
9. I wasted [time/money] on [thing] so you don't have to.
10. Don't [do X] until you've watched this.
11. The worst advice I ever got about [topic].

## Contrarian
12. Unpopular opinion: [belief most people hold] is wrong.
13. Everyone says [common advice]. I did the opposite.
14. [Popular thing] is overrated. Here's what works instead.
15. You don't need [thing everyone thinks you need] to [result].
16. I stopped [doing common thing] and [surprising result].

## Results and proof
17. How I went from [before] to [after] in [time].
18. [Specific number] [result] in [time], and here's exactly how.
19. This one change got me [result].
20. What [result] actually looks like behind the scenes.
21. I tried [thing] for [time]. Here's what happened.

## Curiosity
22. Nobody talks about this part of [topic].
23. The thing I wish I knew before [milestone].
24. This sounds weird, but [unexpected tip].
25. I found out why [problem] keeps happening.
26. There's a reason [frustrating thing] keeps happening, and it's not what you think.
27. [Number] seconds to [result]. Watch.

## Lists and steps
28. [Number] things I'd do if I were starting [topic] today.
29. [Number] [tools/apps/habits] that [result], and they're all free.
30. The [number]-step [routine/system] I use every [day/week].
31. Steal my [template/checklist/routine] for [result].
32. Everything I'd tell my [past self / best friend] about [topic].

## Story
33. [Time] ago I was [low moment]. Today [high moment].
34. The day I almost quit [thing].
35. My [kid/partner/mom] said something that changed how I [do thing].
36. I didn't want to post this, but [honest lead-in].
37. Here's what happened when [unexpected event].

## Questions
38. Why does nobody tell you [truth]?
39. What would you do with [an extra hour / $X / a free weekend]?
40. Are you making this [topic] mistake too?
41. Can you really [result] with [constraint]?

## Relatable and funny
42. Me pretending I have it together while [chaos].
43. Things [who] say vs. what they mean.
44. Tell me you're a [who] without telling me you're a [who].
45. The [number] stages of [relatable process].

## Tutorial
46. Here's how to [result] in [short time], even if [objection].
47. Do this before you [common action].
48. The easiest way to [result] that nobody uses.
49. Save this for the next time you [situation].
50. Watch me [do task] in real time.

## Make it hit harder
- Put the most specific word first ("$4,312" beats "a lot of money").
- Show it, don't only say it: hold up the thing, point at the screen, start mid-action.
- On-screen hook ≤ 7 words; it can differ from the spoken hook.
- Cut every word before the first interesting one.
